Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. Swipe Quest is a single-player game and doesn't require any sign-in or account.

How does the game work?

Swipe to send the ball gliding until it hits an obstacle. Reach the flag in the minimum number of moves to earn 3 stars.

When do ads appear?

A short ad may appear after every 7 completed levels, never during active gameplay. Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an extra hint or a heart refill.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Tap the Privacy settings link on the home screen to review or change your ad consent choices at any time.

What are coins for?

Coins are earned by playing and can be spent on cosmetic items like hats and auras, and on optional power-ups. Everything is pure puzzle fun, with no pay-to-win.
